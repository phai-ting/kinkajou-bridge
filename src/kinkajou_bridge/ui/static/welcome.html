First-time setup

Welcome to Kinkajou Bridge

Connect your 3D printers so Streamer.bot and other tools can react to print events and read live status. This local hub stays on your PC — start by connecting a service or adding a printer.

Printers Services Streamer.bot

What you can do next

  • Services
    Link Bambu Lab cloud once, then pick printers from the account.
  • Printers
    From a connected service or over LAN (Bambu, OctoPrint, and more later).
  • Streamer.bot
    One connection forwards events from every printer across all services and LAN hosts.
  • Use the local API
    Overlays and other apps can call this Bridge on your machine.
